Privacy and cookies

How Artan Records Studio handles account information and your choices.

Effective date: 2026-10-18Last updated: 2026-10-18

Your account

Studio account information is used to provide access, maintain your artist profile and respond to your requests. Creating an account does not grant access to another artist's work or financial records. Profile photographs and unreleased submissions are not public catalogue content.

Cookies and similar technologies

Essential browser storage supports sign-in and security. Your cookie choice is remembered in this browser. Optional appearance storage is used only after Allow; Skip leaves it off. You can revisit Cookies & preferences in the footer. External services apply their own notices when you visit them.

Your choices

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact contact@artanrecords.com. Some records may need to be retained to meet contractual or legal obligations. Account and payment services will be described here when connected; no banking or tax information should be submitted through public forms.
