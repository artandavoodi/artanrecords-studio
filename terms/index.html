Access to Artan Records Studio and artist services.

Accounts and artist access

Use accurate account information and protect your credentials. An account is separate from an artist agreement; label-managed approval determines access to artist workspaces. Do not access another person's records or submit work without permission.

Your work

Submit only material you are entitled to share. A submission is not an agreement to release or distribute a recording. Distribution, rights, royalty splits and payment obligations require a separate confirmed agreement.

Royalties and payments

Listening statistics are not a royalty statement. Earnings, deductions, currencies, reporting periods and payout eligibility depend on verified reports and the applicable agreement. No payout amount or date is promised by an unconnected workspace. Payment or identity onboarding must use the designated secure provider; do not send bank details through public enquiries.

Help and statutory rights

Contact contact@artanrecords.com for access or service questions. These terms do not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Financial services, charges and payment-provider terms must be disclosed before those features are activated.
